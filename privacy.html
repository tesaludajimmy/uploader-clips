Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

Uploader Clips is a private desktop tool used only by the creators of the podcast Delirios & Barbaries with their own TikTok account.

Data we access

  • user.info.basic: the account's open ID and display name, only to confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload: permission to send our own video clips to the connected account's TikTok inbox.

How we store it

The access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, and a list of the clips already sent, are stored only on the computer that runs the tool. They are not shared with anyone, sold or used for advertising or analytics.

Data we do not collect

We do not read followers, messages, videos, comments, contacts or any other information from TikTok, and we do not process data of any other TikTok user.

Deletion

Revoking the tool's access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services permissions) invalidates the tokens; the local files are then deleted.
